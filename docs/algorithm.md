## V7 priority dimensions

- Frequency: 15%
- Persistence: 20%
- Trend: 15%
- Severity: 20%
- Density: 10%
- Network: 20%

These weights are governance choices for the prototype and should not be described as objectively optimal without validation against real municipal outcomes.

Relationship scores indicate directional association, not causality.
